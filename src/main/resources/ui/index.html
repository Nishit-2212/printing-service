Printers

        
Two kinds, and they work differently. Label printers take raw
          commands over a network socket with no driver installed. Document printers
          are whatever this computer already has, and a PDF is rasterised before it reaches them.

    Paper
    
            
    
              
              
              
              
            
    
            
    A rectangle on the loaded paper is the safe
              reading: nothing can ask a thermal printer to feed stock it does not have. Only choose
              "the paper itself" for a profile whose output was calibrated under QZ Tray.
    

            
    Margins
    
            
    
              
              
              
              
            
    
            
    Java gives every page a one-inch inset by default, so zero here means
              genuine full bleed rather than a label crushed into the middle of the media.
    

            
    Rendering
    
            
    
              
              
              
              
              
            
    
            
    Leaving orientation on auto is not laziness: a landscape invoice
              that has to be rotated onto a portrait strip is detected from the PDF's own page box,
              and forcing portrait here is what silently prints it upright.

          another, the same page twice on two machines.

      Panel defaults
      
            
            
            
            
      
              
              
            
      
            
      The confirm wait is an upper bound, not a delay: a job settles the moment
              the printer finishes or refuses. Set it to 0 to stop waiting for confirmation at all —
              faster, and you stop being told whether anything came out.
      
            
            
      On by default. Turn it off only if this station's printer is busy enough
              that drawing a preview has to wait behind a print — the preview is rendered by the
              printer's own lane, which is what makes it trustworthy.
